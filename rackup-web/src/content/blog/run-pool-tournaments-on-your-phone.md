# Run pool tournaments on your phone

A hall tournament should not depend on a laptop at the counter, a paper bracket that tears, and one person who “has the sheet.” RackUp puts the event on **[Tournaments](/tournaments)** so the director, the players, and the TV in the corner are looking at the same bracket.

This is RackUp by Rack of Champions — the player network — not a clipboard app that borrowed the name.

## Formats the house actually uses

- **Single elimination** — lose and go home. Fast, brutal, easy to explain.
- **Double elimination** — the back door. Players stay in until they don’t.
- **Swiss** — pair by record, no instant death, good when the field is big and the night is long.
- **Chip races** — when the house wants a different kind of pressure than a race-to-7.

You register from the phone. You see the bracket update without standing over the director’s shoulder.

## What “on your phone” means

Directors can stand up. Players can sit at a table and still know who they play next. The public **TV board** (`/tournaments/:id/tv`) is built for a hall display — no app chrome, no “please log in” wall in front of the room.

That TV route is public on purpose. The house should be able to throw it on a screen. Everyone else still uses the app shell for register, scores, and chat.

## A simple run of show

1. Create or open the event on **[Tournaments](/tournaments)** (also reachable from **[Play](/play)**).
2. Players register from their own phones — stop collecting names on a napkin.
3. Start the bracket. Pairings go out. Tables fill.
4. Report results as matches end. The next round does not wait on a clipboard.
5. Put the TV board on the house screen if you have one.

If someone no-shows, the director still has a bracket they can work — not a Facebook event with 40 “going” and nine bodies.

## How this sits next to money sets

A tournament is a field. A money set is a pair. Book one-off stakes on **[Play](/play)** with dual confirm. Run a house event on Tournaments. Same network, different shape.

Players who showed up via **[Find](/find)** or **[Halls](/halls)** should be able to enter without a second product. That is the point of one player card.

## What we will not fake

No fake sponsor banners. No pretend “102 players registered” to juice a landing page. If the field is eight, the bracket is eight. The room already knows.

## Practice does not stop because you lost first round

Bust out, then hit **[Coach](/coach)** or a drill on **[Shots](/shots)** instead of only posting about the draw. The catalogue does not care that you ran into the house champion in round one.
