# Find pool players near me

The oldest problem in pool is not a bank shot. It is **finding a game tonight** without spamming a group chat and hoping someone is already in the room.

RackUp matchmaking on **[Find](/find)** is built for that: a live looking board of players near you, filtered by game and stakes, tied to real halls on the Rack of Champions network.

## Why “near me” has to mean the hall

A worldwide “challenge anyone” button is a toy. You play on a table, in a room, at a time. RackUp keeps discovery local on purpose.

- Players go **looking** with a game (8-ball, 9-ball, 10-ball, one-pocket) and a stakes band (casual, money, action).
- You see who is actually on the board — not a ghosted “online” dot from last week.
- You can challenge, message, or add someone, then take it to **[Halls](/halls)** or **[Play](/play)**.

If the room is quiet, you still have a move: **go live yourself**. The board is a two-way street. Hiding in the parking lot and waiting for the universe to text you is how nights die.

## How to use Find

1. Open **[Find](/find)** and let location settle (or set the map origin if you are scouting a different neighborhood).
2. Filter game and stakes so you are not matching a race-to-7 action player when you wanted a casual 8-ball.
3. Read the looking cards — rating, game, hall context when it is there.
4. Challenge or DM. If you want the house to see you, tap go live and stay on the board.

Matchmaking can also search a radius and wait for a confirm. That is for when you want the app to do the tapping while you rack. Either way, the point is a **table**, not a leaderboard screenshot.

## Pair it with Hall Pulse

A looking player with no hall is only half a plan. Check **[Halls](/halls)** in the same session:

- Who is already checked in.
- Whether the room is quiet, moderate, or busy.
- Whether the drive is worth it before you leave the house.

Find gets you a name. Halls get you a door. Together they replace “anyone out?” in a thread of 40 people who are already asleep.

## Casual, money, and everything in between

Not every search is a stake set. Plenty of players want a hit, a coach session afterward, or a tournament the hall is running that night.

- Casual looking is first-class. You are not second-rate because you did not post a number.
- If money is on it, take the set to **[Play](/play)** so the stake and the result are dual-confirmed — not a rumor.
- If the house is bracketing up, skip the hunt and join **[Tournaments](/tournaments)**.

## What Find is not

It is not a dating app with a cue. It is not a global ELO casino. It is not the other “RackUp” scorekeeping tools that share a name and do not live on rackofchampions.com.

RackUp by Rack of Champions is the **player network**. Find is how that network stops being theoretical.
