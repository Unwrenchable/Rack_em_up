# How pool hall check-in works

A pool hall can look closed from the parking lot and be three deep on the back tables. It can also look busy on Instagram and be two regulars and a broken jukebox. **Check-in** is how RackUp tells those nights apart.

On **[Halls](/halls)**, you pick a room, check in, and feed **Hall Pulse** — the live read of who is there and how the house feels. That pulse is what **[Find](/find)** and the home board are trying to show you before you waste a tank of gas.

## What a check-in actually does

Check-in is a player saying: *I am in this room right now.*

- The hall’s active player count moves.
- Your name can show on the room’s live list.
- Pulse status shifts between quiet, moderate, and busy as people arrive and leave.
- Friends and looking players can see that the house has a pulse, not a rumor.

It is not a loyalty punch card. It is not a review. It is presence.

## Check in, play, check out

1. Open **[Halls](/halls)** and find the room — map, list, or search.
2. Check in when you are actually there. Checking in from the couch makes Pulse a liar.
3. Play your sets. Book money on **[Play](/play)** if that is the night. Join a bracket on **[Tournaments](/tournaments)** if the house is running one.
4. Check out when you leave. The next player driving over deserves a clean board.

Verified halls (the ones the network trusts as real rooms) are the ones you want to lean on. Anyone can claim a name; verification is how Rack of Champions keeps the map from filling with ghost bars.

## Why Pulse beats a group chat

Group chats do not expire. Someone said “headed to the hall” at 6:10 and is still on the couch at 9. Check-in expires with your body: you are in, or you are out.

That is the whole product bet. **Live** means live. Demo mode is labeled when the API is down. We do not paint fake crowds on a dark room to make the app look busy.

## Using halls with Find

A good night is usually two screens:

- **[Find](/find)** — who wants a game, what they play, what they want on the set.
- **[Halls](/halls)** — which door those people are behind.

If you are looking and not checked in, you are asking the city to guess. Check in, go looking, and let the board do what the group chat never could.

## Privacy, in plain language

Other players on the network can see that a hall has people. That is the point of Pulse. You are not broadcasting a street address that was not already on the hall pin. If you do not want to be seen, do not check in — and do not expect the room to magically fill around you.

## After the room

Check-in is for the night. Practice is for the morning. **[Coach](/coach)** and **[Shots](/shots)** are still there when the last table is covered.
