# Money sets and stake matches

A money set is only as honest as the **result**. Handshake scores, disappearing chats, and “I thought we were playing to 5” are how rooms get ugly.

**[Play](/play)** is where RackUp puts the set on the board: game, race, opponent, hall, stake — then **both players confirm** when it is over. That dual confirm is the point. It is not a bank wire. It is not a sportsbook. It is a record the table can live with.

## What you book

On Play you create a money match with the pieces that actually matter:

- **Game** — 8-ball, 9-ball, and the rest of the house menu.
- **Race** — so “one more” cannot rewrite the night.
- **Opponent** — a friend, a Find match, someone already in the room.
- **Hall** — so the set is tied to a door, not a parking lot.
- **Stake** — the number you both already agreed on.

Optional livestream URL if the house (or you) is putting the set on a stream. That is a link, not a fake “watch live” ad.

## Dual confirm, in one sentence

When the set ends, each side reports the score. The match is complete when **both reports agree**. If they do not, it is disputed — not silently overwritten by whoever opened the app first.

That is the trust model. Rack of Champions would rather show a dispute than pretend a ghost win is official.

## How a stake night usually runs

1. Find the player on **[Find](/find)** or in the hall you [checked into](/halls).
2. Book the set on **[Play](/play)** before the first rack, not after the argument.
3. Play. Keep your own head. The app is not a referee with a whistle.
4. Both confirm. Then the ledger and the player cards can move.

If you wanted a tournament instead of a one-off set, skip the private stake and enter **[Tournaments](/tournaments)**. Different structure, same phone.

## What this is not

- **Not escrow.** Dual confirm is agreement, not a held bankroll. Settle how your room already settles — the app keeps the result from turning into folklore.
- **Not a scorekeeping-only RackUp.** Other products named RackUp count frames. This RackUp is the **network**: who you played, for what, and whether both sides signed the card.
- **Not hidden action.** Stakes are explicit. Casual players can stay on Find and Coach without ever opening a money match.

## Disputes

If the scores do not match, the set is disputed. That is uncomfortable and correct. Talk at the table, correct the report, or leave it flagged. Silent “edits” are how people stop playing you.

## After the set

Log the night, then get better. **[Coach](/coach)** and **[Shots](/shots)** are still on the home screen when the stake is done.
