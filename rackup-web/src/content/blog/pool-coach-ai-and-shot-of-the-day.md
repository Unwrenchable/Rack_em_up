# Pool Coach AI and Shot of the Day

Most players already know they should practice. They do not have a **today** — a specific shot, a reason to rack, and a way to notice if they are actually improving.

**[Coach](/coach)** is that today: **Shot of the Day**, a short drill list, and **Coach AI** when you want notes on a shot or a clip. **[Shots](/shots)** is the deeper catalogue when you want jumps, massés, combos, banks, and kicks on purpose — not whatever the last YouTube autoplay served.

## Shot of the Day

SOTD is one diagrammed shot with a job. Make it, mark it, keep a streak if you are that kind of stubborn. It is not a 40-minute lesson. It is a reason to get to a table before the night turns into only gambling or only scrolling.

The maps are built for this app — ghost ball, jump line, massé curve — not a stock PNG of a table. If a shot looks wrong, that is a bug we fix, not “content.”

## Coach AI

Coach AI is for when the miss has a story: a clip, a note, a game (9-ball, whatever you were on). You send the context; you get analysis back. It is a coach that does not need to be at your hall at 1 a.m.

It will not secretly re-rack the balls for you. It will not replace a human instructor who can see your elbow. It will give you something more useful than “hit center ball” after you already knew that.

Use it after a set on **[Play](/play)** or after you bust out of **[Tournaments](/tournaments)**. The point is to turn a loss into a next shot, not a mood.

## Daily drills

Coach also lists today’s drills so you are not inventing a practice plan in the parking lot. Complete them, or don’t — the streak is for you, not for a public humiliation feed.

When you want a type of shot on demand, leave the daily list and open **[Shots](/shots)**. That catalogue is where [jump, massé, and combo work](/blog/jump-masse-combo-drills-catalogue) lives in bulk.

## How this fits the rest of RackUp

Training that never meets a live player is homework. RackUp is still a **network**:

- Take the improved stroke to **[Find](/find)**.
- Check into the hall on **[Halls](/halls)** and use the table that is actually open.
- Put the match on **[Play](/play)** if the night has a number on it.

Coach is the morning. The board is the night.

## Not a scorekeeper, not a content farm

RackUp by Rack of Champions is not “AI articles about pool” with a shop link. The shots are in the app. The coach is in the app. Other products named RackUp that only keep score are doing a different job — they can count a race; they cannot give you Shot of the Day on this network.
