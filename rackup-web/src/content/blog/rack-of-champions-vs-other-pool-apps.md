# Rack of Champions vs other pool apps

Search “RackUp” and you will not get one product. That is the first thing to say out loud.

**RackUp by Rack of Champions** is the player network at [rackofchampions.com](https://www.rackofchampions.com/). It is built to [find players](/find), [check into halls](/halls), [book money sets](/play), [run tournaments](/tournaments), and [train](/coach). Other billiard apps — including some that also use the RackUp name — are doing a different job.

This is a map, not a roast.

## Scorekeepers named RackUp (and similar)

Frame counters, race trackers, and league sheet helpers are useful at the table. They answer: *what is the score?*

They do not answer: *who is looking near me, is the hall alive, did both of us sign this stake, what is today’s shot?*

If all you need is a tally, a dedicated scorekeeper is fine. If you need a **network**, you are in the wrong listing when the URL is not rackofchampions.com. Our own scorekeeping exists as part of a match or a tournament TV board — it is not the whole product.

## Group chats and “anyone playing?”

Facebook groups, iMessage threads, and hall pages are how most rooms still fill. They are also how nights stall: stale “omw,” no filters, no hall pulse, no result that both players own.

RackUp is opinionated about that:

- **[Find](/find)** is a looking board, not a wall of comments.
- **[Halls](/halls)** is check-in, not a photo from Saturday.
- **[Play](/play)** dual-confirms a money set so the score cannot dissolve in the parking lot.

You can still text. You should not have to rely on text.

## Rating sites and lookup tools

Fargo-style lookups and master lists tell you a number. RackUp will show rating on a **player card** because the card is how you recognize someone on Find and in a bracket. The number is not the app. The board is the app.

## Generic sports / “find a game” platforms

City-wide pickup apps optimize for soccer fields and rec leagues. Pool has halls, races, stakes, and a different kind of etiquette. A pin on a map that says “billiards” is not Hall Pulse. A bracket tool that does not know double elim from a chip race will fight the director all night.

RackUp stays inside this sport: 8-ball and 9-ball on **Find**, Pulse on **Halls**, dual confirm on **Play**, SE/DE/Swiss/chips on **Tournaments**, diagrams on **[Shots](/shots)**.

## Content apps and YouTube coaches

There is more pool instruction on the internet than anyone will ever finish. **[Coach](/coach)** is narrower: Shot of the Day, drills, Coach AI on *your* miss. **[Shots](/shots)** is a catalogue you can open next to the table. We are not trying to be a media company with a shop.
